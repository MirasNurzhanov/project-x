Aura puts you in touch with people who are physically near you. That makes it more useful than an app where everyone is a continent away, and it makes the rules matter more. These Guidelines are part of the Terms of Use.

Zero tolerance

Objectionable content and abusive users are not tolerated on Aura. We remove content that breaks these Guidelines and we suspend or ban the accounts behind it. Serious violations are permanent on the first offence.

Not allowed, ever

Sexual content involving minors

Any sexual content involving a person under 18, in any form, real or drawn. Any attempt to contact a minor for a sexual purpose. This results in immediate permanent removal and is reported to the relevant authorities. There is no appeal.

Harassment, threats and stalking

Targeting a person with abuse, threatening violence, repeatedly contacting somebody who has asked you to stop, or using Aura's proximity features to follow, watch or turn up on somebody.

Hate speech

Attacking or dehumanising people for their race, ethnicity, national origin, religion, caste, sexual orientation, sex, gender identity, disability or serious illness. This includes slurs, and includes "jokes".

Sexual content and nudity

Aura is not a place for pornography or sexual solicitation. Do not post explicit imagery, and do not solicit sex, whether or not money is involved.

Violence and graphic content

Content glorifying violence, gore posted for shock, or anything that promotes a violent act.

Self-harm and suicide

Do not encourage self-harm, and do not tell anybody to hurt themselves. If you are struggling, please contact a local crisis line — and if somebody on Aura is in danger, report it and contact emergency services.

Impersonation and deception

Pretending to be another person or an organisation, or using someone else's photographs as your own.

Spam, scams and money

Bulk or repetitive posting, advertising, phishing, crypto schemes, and any request for money, gift cards or account credentials. Nobody you meet on Aura has a legitimate reason to ask you for money.

Other people's private information

Do not publish anybody's home address, phone number, workplace, or anything else that would let a stranger find them offline.

How this is enforced

  • Automated screening. Text posted to Aura is screened before it is published. Content in the categories above is refused outright; borderline content is held back and shown to a moderator before anybody else sees it.
  • Reports. Every Swift, story, comment, message and profile carries a report control. Reports are reviewed by a person, normally within 24 hours. Content reported independently by several people is hidden while it waits.
  • Blocking. You can block any account from its profile or from a chat. Blocking is immediate and mutual: they disappear from your feed, your map, your search results and your messages, and you disappear from theirs.
  • Consequences. Content is removed. Accounts are suspended, and banned permanently for serious or repeated violations. We keep a record of every moderation decision and who made it.

Meeting people safely

  • Meet in a public, populated place the first time, in daylight.
  • Tell a friend where you are going, who you are meeting and when you expect to be back.
  • Arrange your own transport, so leaving is entirely your decision.
  • Keep your home street, school, workplace and daily routine out of your profile.
  • Trust your instincts. If something feels wrong, leave. You owe a stranger no explanation.
  • If you are in immediate danger, contact emergency services first. Reporting to us can wait.

Reporting to us

Use the report control in the app — it reaches the moderation queue directly and carries the context we need. For anything that does not fit, or to appeal a decision, write to {{ contact_email }}.
