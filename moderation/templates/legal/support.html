Common questions

Who can see where I am?

Friends you have accepted see you on the map. Anybody else sees you only while you are checked in. Ghost Mode, in Settings › Privacy & safety, hides you from everyone, friends included. More in the Privacy page.

How do I stop a friend's zone music?

Turn off Settings › Play friends' zone music.

How do I block someone?

Open their profile or anything they posted and choose Block. You can unblock in Settings › Blocked users.

How do I delete my account?

Settings › Delete account. Your account is disabled at once and permanently deleted {{ grace_days }} days later. Messages you sent to others and safety records are kept, as the Privacy page explains.

I can't sign in

Email us from the address on your account and say how you signed up (email, Google or Apple).
